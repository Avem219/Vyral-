The FUO story

Rooted in purpose.
Driven by possibility.

Federal University Otuoke is a federal government-owned university in Otuoke, Ogbia Local Government Area of Bayelsa State, Nigeria.

Established in February 2011, the university began academic activities in 2012. Its mission is to generate, disseminate, preserve and apply knowledge through teaching, research, innovation and service.
